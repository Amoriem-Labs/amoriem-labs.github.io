Games

Amoriem Labs makes two games a year and aims to publish them on online platforms for free. We run a yearly pitch process, where anyone can pitch their ideas to be created by the club.

2025-2026 2024-2025 2023-2024 Pre-2023

2025-2026

27 Photos

27 Photos is a walking-simulator/puzzle game where you must uncover the story of the strange building you start in and the nameless character you play. Take pictures to reveal information about your surroundings, but be sure not to use all 27 of them!

Made by the Photos team, led by Chris Shia.

Demo is out now!

Cursed Grounds

Cursed Grounds is a dungeon-crawler/restaurant simulator! Set in a fantasy world, you must traverse the dungeons to gather ingredients from strange creatures to create new items for your menu!

Made by the Dungeon team, led by Maryeva Gonzalez and Sam Johnson-Noya.

Demo is out now!

2024-2025

Boola Dash

Boola Dash is a side-scrolling platformer where you play as Handsome Dan running across Yale trying to avoid obstacles like benches, cones, and students! Play to get on our leaderboard, where the best Resco will win! This game was made in collaboration with the Alumni Fund at Yale.

Made by the Alumni Game team, led by Maryeva Gonzalez. See full credits on itch.io!

Encore!

Begin your career at the Multiversal Institute for the Rhythmic Arts (MIRA), an interdimensional school where music, magic, and friendship collide. Recruit new friends from across realities and times to form a band that will rival the ages and unlock the power of rhythm with your bonds. Can your band make the audience scream Encore!?

Made by the Rhythm/Visual Novel Game team, led by Chris Shia. See full credits on itch.io!

Glyphbound

Glyphbound is a roguelite deckbuilder that centers around scribing spells to craft crazy combos! Play as a time-travelling wizard tasked with saving the world from a growing corruption.

Made by the Magic team, led by Jeffrey Tan. See full credits on itch.io!

Conquer Chiron

Conquer Chiron is a psychological horror game that plays on the fear of AI in the post-modern world. Play as a pilot who is trapped with the deviant AI assistant Chiron. Can you survive the onslaught of Chiron’s assassination attempts?

Made by Victor Liu, Will Liu, Grace Halak, and Jacob Leshnower.

2023-2024

Echoes of Eternity

A minute can last seconds here… or an eternity. Stuck in an unstable realm, you battle ever-growing hordes of enemies to survive — and you can travel through time to aid your future self and receive help from your past self. An endless, arcade-style test of how well you traverse the fourth dimension.

Made by Victor Liu, Jeffrey Tan, Will Liu, Grace Halak, and Jacob Leshnower.

Rosefield Requiem

Six months ago, a pop star from the small, quaint town of Rosefield died from apparent suicide. However, the clues don’t seem to add up, indicating that a murderer is hiding among the townsfolks. Every choice matters in this murder mystery and dating sim visual novel!

Made by the Visual Novel team, led by Chris Shia. See full credits on itch.io!

Diploma Dash

Diploma Dash is a fast-paced platformer game made by Amoriem Labs in the school year 2023-2024. You are a Yale college senior who has unwisely decided to major in every discipline and needs to rush his last week of classes to finish all his course requirements. Collect all your assignments and make it to the end in order to graduate on time!

Made by the Platformer team. Led by Victor Liu, with full credits on itch.io!

Pre-2023

Planet 112

After crash landing on an unknown planet, you find yourself in a mysterious alien ecosystem. Unravel the secrets of this planet and plant your way through the lands to go back home!

Made by Victor Liu, Xien Chen, Jesse Chen, Will Liu, Jacob Feit Mann, Jessie Hwang, Adrian Hall, Nick He, Win Hlaing, Harry Hwang, Lucas Huang, and other collaborators.

Play Now

Bulldog Bash

Choose your favorite between former dean Marvin Chun and current Yale president Peter Salovey in this arcade-style 1v1 fighting game!

Made by Christie Yu, Eric Xue, Eric Doddy, Jack Li, Adrian Hall, Nick He, Ethan Pesikoff, Murtaza Javaid, Luc Ta, and other collaborators.
