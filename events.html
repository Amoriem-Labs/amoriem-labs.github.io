Mental Health in Gaming

A roundtable and game night with the SHARE Center on online gaming spaces, bystander intervention, and mental health — with Panera catering and a round of Overcooked to close things out.

Indigenous Voices

A collaboration with Professor Tarren Andrews’ class: gameplay demos of Skabma and Never Alone, followed by a discussion on Indigenous storytelling in games.

XR Pediatrics
Milk & Cookies

A midterms study break with milk, cookies, and good vibes — a low-key reset before finals season.

Valentine's Game Night

Members shared their favorite romance and co-op games over a night of themed treats and friendly competition.

Halloween Game Night

A spooky evening of costumes, horror games, and community as members celebrated the season together.

Alumni in Games Panel

A virtual Q&A with Amoriem alumni now working in the games industry — how they broke in, and what the day-to-day is really like.
