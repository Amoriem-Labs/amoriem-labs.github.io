Games About Events Team

Amoriem Labs (Yale Undergraduate Game Developers) is Yale’s only game development club! Our members include designers, programmers, artists, writers, composers, and more, working together to create and publish original indie games.

Beyond game development, we foster a creative community through game jams, guest speakers, and social events, providing students with opportunities to explore game design, build skills, and collaborate on projects that bring ideas to life. Amoriem Labs is open to all members of the Yale community, regardless of experience level, and has no application process.

Club Games

Amoriem Labs develops two games a year, guided by an open pitch process! Anyone can pitch their unique ideas, and the club develops throughout the school year. We meet every Sunday to gather ideas, work together, and build community.

We have made games from platformers to visual novels to rhythm games to fighting beat-em-ups. Pitch your best ideas and make your ideas come true!
